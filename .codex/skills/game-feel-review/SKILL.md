Read root AGENTS.md and the issue. Before editing, play the relevant flow and record its friction; after editing, repeat that same flow. Use playtest-capture for rendered evidence. If the engine cannot run, distinguish source review from unverified playthroughs.

Walk once as a first-time player with no external instructions, and once as a player with ten hours of experience (replay/skip, familiar shortcuts, old-save state). Record concrete moments of confusion, boredom, pressure or administrative work, and what the player can do next.

For each F principle record PASS, FAIL, PARTIAL or N/A, with a screen/step or test reference and remaining problem:

| Principle | Check |
|---|---|
| F1 | First use has guided, untimed, unscored, safe practice; one target highlighted; replay and skip work. |
| F2 | Default play tolerates waiting; any timing is explicitly opt-in; normal pay remains normal. |
| F3 | Only available content is shown; at most the next unlock is previewed. |
| F4 | One primary next action; advanced choices are folded away. |
| F5 | Repetitive administration can be delegated with reasonable results and manual recovery. |
| F6 | Cards have at most two explanatory lines and three numbers; details live under help. |
| F7 | Wrong actions explain recovery; retry works; cancellation, old saves and completed/impossible states cannot lock progress. |
| F8 | Information and decoration are quiet; emphasis signals a current action. |

Use progressive disclosure, readable feedback/juice and flow (difficulty follows ability). Prefer meaningful choices to forms. Evaluate delight as well as correctness: can the player see what changed and feel successful? Keep the assessment within the ticket; inherited issues remain explicit rather than becoming an unsolicited redesign.

Attach before/after F results, both player perspectives, screenshot references, tested save/ledger invariants and open doubts to the PR. Do not call a source inspection a successful rendered playtest.
