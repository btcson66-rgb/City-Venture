# Real Estate and Residential

Implemented `real_estate`, reachable at `residential` through M5 Loop or the bidirectional Old Town walking exit. Four buildings: `maple_court`, `birch_row`, `harlow_finch`, and `lot7`. Maple has six purchaseable units, Birch has three; these are shared `properties` data, not a parallel housing catalog. `realty_office` is the $2,200/month brokerage lease. Diane Harlow (`diane_harlow`) introduces matching; Ruiz Construction (`ruiz`) explains paid development. Marcus retains existing business-loan conversations and adds a mortgage explanation and bank interactable. Generated tenants have names, types, credit, arrears and damage risks from economy data. Employee role `realty_agent` assists matching and uses standard recruitment/payroll.

Growth starts with the City Hall brokerage exam: fee plus three-day processing through Compliance's saved permit queue. Matchmaker lists owner mandates (sale/rent, reserve price, rooms, district) opposite buyer/tenant budget, rooms and urgency. Hard/balanced/soft negotiation trades commission against acceptance. Paid viewings take game time; failed deals still cost cash. A successful deal creates and completes a Jobs contract; sales commissions are 2–3%, rental commissions one month's rent. No passive daily brokerage income.

Ownership uses Bank property-secured mortgages: 20–30% down, 20–30 years, base-linked interest, monthly principal amortization and the existing late/default/closure process. Property equity increases lending capacity without subtracting mortgage principal twice. Outbound Ruiz jobs do not inflate signed receivable collateral. Property historical book cost and RealEstateMarket monthly random-walk market value remain separate; price appreciation creates no cash revenue.

Rent pricing and minimum tenant credit affect vacancy. Actual generated tenants must pass screening. Each monthly tenancy invoice posts cash or receivables, with possible damage repair and later collection/write-off; taxes and management are charged while empty as well. Basic/Modern/Premium renovation costs $15k/$35k/$60k, takes time, can overrun and increases potential rent and market value; unfinished renovation cannot be leased or sold.

After owning a rental, apply at City Hall for the Lot 7 building permit: fee and processing, rather than a quiz. Choose 6/10/15 units and 60/90/120 days, for $1.2M/$2M/$3M. Name the building (1–32 characters). Jobs holds an outbound Ruiz contract; monthly paid milestones capitalize construction in progress. Cash shortages pause, contractor delays and neighbor mediation move completion; extra material cost must also be paid before completion. Completed units can be sold or leased separately, or all leased as one building portfolio. The completed named landmark is saved independently from company ownership, appears as a real facade and in the city-map Residential panel, and remains after company closure/restart.

Crises: `real_estate_rate`, `real_estate_arrears`, `real_estate_delay`, `real_estate_neighbors`. Each active event routes through the registry and changes financial or scheduled state. All balancing numbers live in `economy/real_estate.json`. Data is lazy for old saves; property, mortgages, tenant invoices, renovation, permits and project milestone queues persist. Closure auctions property/work once, cancels tenant/work callbacks and lets the core settle loan debts and receivables.

See [art backlog](90_codex_art_backlog.md) for named facade requests. Existing apartment/rowhouse/lettings/warehouse facades and actual furnished interiors serve as supported fallbacks. New screens have help/glossary and stable names; Company OS and Business Board use registry descriptors.
