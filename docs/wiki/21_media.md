# Media / Advertising and University

Implemented `media` in active `university`, connected to M1 metro. Buildings `aurelia_university`, `the_loft` and `campus_radio` have furnished interiors and named fallbacks. `loft_office` costs $1,800/month. NPCs `imani_cole` (Dr. Imani Cole), `kai_morgan` and `student_intern` (Nia Park) explain real client briefs, creator channels and recruitment. Roles `media_designer`, `media_buyer` and `media_intern` use standard employer registration, applicants and weekly payroll ($950/$900/$350).

Jobs briefs include $5k–80k budgets, goal, audience and deadline. Creative Pitch has three actual card decisions: slogan, visual and tone, with preferences disclosed by briefs and mentor. Creative production and proposals cost cash and time; proposals can lose. Creative quality and hired skills affect CTR. Campaign Mixer normalizes seven channel shares to 100%; CPM, audience composition and exponential saturation determine marginal impressions. Daily campaigns record impressions, relevant reach, clicks and conversions; mid-campaign changes apply to subsequent purchases. Paused campaigns occupy capacity and require funding to resume.

Client deposits are deferred liabilities. Daily purchases post actual costs after 12% supplier rebate (15% with buyer). Completion invoices delivered media scope plus fixed service fee and earned KPI bonus through Jobs, with Net 30 collection. Missing KPI reduces reputation and future brief number/budget. Cancellation refunds unused advances and invoices only completed work. No passive daily agency income.

Group campaigns post cost-price advertising to the target segment, marked internal, with no service markup or rebate. Measured conversions feed Cafe.ads_factor and Ecommerce.demand_mult. Hotel eligibility is gated until the hotel module is available; its hook must consume the same saved demand boost when implemented.

Solo capacity one; completed two campaigns plus designer/buyer unlock agency capacity three. Buying Campus Radio uses depreciating Assets, real maintenance/failure and bank collateral. Owned media sells finite daily impression inventory using Jobs, grows audience through measured work and has upkeep even without sales. Repeated sales cannot reuse a delivered contract. Crises `media_pr`, `media_client`, `media_price` change creative quality, cancellation and CPM; events route through the industry registry. Save/load keeps briefs, mixes, reports, Jobs, assets, boosts and schedules; old saves initialize lazily and closure cancels callbacks/refunds once.

Company OS, Business Board, glossary and help use the shared registry. Numeric assumptions live in economy/media.json. See 90_codex_art_backlog.md for facade requests; existing civic, co-work and office art supplies complete supported fallbacks.
